## Review Focus

- A lock issue of `0` or `0104` reaches `live.sh beat`: nothing is published, no `live:` line (Task 4).
- A signal planted with a `beat` far in the future (clock skew, or hostile): not listed; a `beat` a minute ahead still is (Task 4).
- `CODEO_CLEANUP_REMOVAL` exported by accident, with or without an overlay: `cleanup.sh` prints what it printed without it (Task 2).
- `/run/.containerenv` present on a host that also has `/.dockerenv`, or neither: the role is the same as for one marker; with neither it stays `unguarded` (Task 1).
- A refused DELETE (404 `Not Found`) is reported as `could not clear`, while 422 `Reference does not exist` stays silent (Task 5).
